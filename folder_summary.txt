        One Village – One Didi
        “One Didi – One Village, A New Identity of Self-Reliance”
        “One Village – One Didi” is a social and economic empowerment initiative designed to promote women entrepreneurship, self-reliance, and sustainable development in rural India.
        Under this initiative, one active and capable woman — a “Didi” — is identified from each village to serve as a local leader, facilitator, and bridge between rural women and wider business opportunities.
        The Role of a Didi
        Each selected Didi works closely with women, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), local entrepreneurs, and community members within her village. She helps identify local skills, products, and business potential while encouraging women to participate in income-generating activities.
        Most importantly, the Didi acts as a bridge between the village and the market, helping locally produced and women-made products reach the right customers, markets, and business networks at fair value.
        Our Objectives
        Through the One Village – One Didi initiative, we aim to:
        Promote women entrepreneurship and leadership at the grassroots level
        Create new opportunities for self-employment and income generation
        Connect rural and women-made products with larger markets
        Support Self-Help Groups and women entrepreneurs
        Encourage the development of micro and small businesses
        Promote digital, financial, and business awareness
        Create sustainable employment opportunities within villages
        Facilitate market connections from local to national and potentially international markets
        Our Vision
        Our vision goes beyond empowering one woman. We believe that one empowered woman can become a catalyst for the economic transformation of an entire village.
        When one Didi grows, she creates opportunities for many more women, families, entrepreneurs, and communities to grow with her.
        One Didi → One Group → One Enterprise → One Self-Reliant Village
        One Didi – One Village
        A New Identity of Self-Reliance.
